# Cross-Border Load Checklist

The Customs & Border Desk (border@maplefreight.example, 905-555-0155, 24/7) files all eManifests. **Drivers never file eManifests themselves.** Your job is to make sure the paperwork in the cab matches what was filed.

## 1. Before you leave the yard — southbound (Canada → US)

- [ ] Passport or FAST card is valid and on you.
- [ ] You have the **ACE lead sheet** from the Border Desk, showing the trip number and shipment control numbers (SCNs). Barcode is readable.
- [ ] Bill of lading and commercial invoice for every shipment on the trailer.
- [ ] Customs broker for each shipment is listed on the lead sheet. If it says "TBD", do not leave — call the Border Desk.
- [ ] Seal number on the trailer matches the seal number on the lead sheet.
- [ ] Trailer and tractor plates match the lead sheet.
- [ ] Hours: you have enough available hours under **US** rules for the US leg. See C01, section 5.
- [ ] Border Desk has confirmed in the driver app: "ACE accepted".

## 2. Before you leave — northbound (US → Canada)

- [ ] Passport or FAST card.
- [ ] **ACI lead sheet** with trip number and cargo control numbers (CCNs).
- [ ] Bill of lading and commercial invoice for every shipment.
- [ ] Border Desk has confirmed: "ACI accepted".

## 3. At the border

- Hand the officer the lead sheet first.
- Answer questions truthfully and briefly. If asked about a shipment you don't know about, say so and give the Border Desk number.
- If you are referred to secondary inspection, tell Dispatch in the driver app. Waiting time is on-duty time — log it.

## 4. Never

- Never cross with a shipment that is not on the lead sheet.
- Never carry anything for anyone outside the bill of lading — no packages, envelopes, or "favours".
- Never cross if the seal is broken or missing. Call the Border Desk.

## 5. Common reasons a load is held

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| eManifest not on file | Call Border Desk before crossing |
| Seal number mismatch | Do not cross; call Border Desk |
| Missing commercial invoice | Call Border Desk; the shipper must send it |
| Broker not notified | Border Desk contacts the broker |
| Driver document expired | Do not cross; call Dispatch |
