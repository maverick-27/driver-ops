# Maple Freight Inc. — Driver Handbook

## 1. About this handbook

This handbook applies to every company driver and lease operator hauling under Maple Freight's CVOR and US DOT number. Where this handbook is stricter than federal, provincial or state law, follow this handbook. Where the law is stricter, follow the law.

Questions about this handbook go to Safety & Compliance (safety@maplefreight.example, 905-555-0140, Monday–Friday 7:00–17:00).

## 3. Yard

Main yard and terminal: Brampton, Ontario. The gate is staffed 24/7. Drivers must badge in and out. Trailers may not be dropped outside the marked drop lot without Dispatch approval.

## 4. Licensing and documents you must carry

Every driver must carry, at all times on duty:

- A valid licence of the correct class for the vehicle (Class A for tractor-trailers in Ontario), and a valid medical certificate where required.
- The company CVOR certificate copy (kept in the cab binder).
- Vehicle permit, insurance card, and IRP cab card (in the cab binder).
- The current daily inspection report for the tractor and trailer.
- Access to the ELD and its user guide (in the cab binder and the driver app).
- For cross-border work: a valid passport or FAST card, plus the trip's lead sheet. See C03 Cross-Border Load Checklist.

A driver who is missing a required document must not move the truck. Call Dispatch.

## 5. Hours of service — company rules

All drivers must follow the hours-of-service rules that apply to where they are driving: Canadian federal rules north of the border, and FMCSA rules in the United States.

Company rules on top of the law:

- Plan every trip so you arrive with **at least 1 hour of available driving time remaining**. If traffic or weather means you will run out of hours, tell Dispatch as soon as you know. Dispatch will re-plan; you will never be disciplined for stopping legally.
- No driver may be asked, and no driver may agree, to drive beyond legal limits. Report any such request to Safety & Compliance. Reports are confidential.
- Log all on-duty time, including loading, waiting at docks, fuelling, and inspections. "Off the clock" yard work is not allowed.

## 6. ELD

Every tractor has an ELD. Drivers must log in at the start of each shift and log out at the end. Do not share login credentials. If the ELD malfunctions, follow C04 Dispatch SOP, section 6.

## 7. Daily inspections

A daily (pre-trip) inspection of the tractor and every trailer is required before the first trip of the day, recorded in the driver app. Any defect must be recorded. Do not drive with a major defect; report it to Dispatch and the Breakdown desk.

## 8. Cell phones and distraction

Hand-held phone use while driving is prohibited. Use the hands-free mount only. Do not read or type messages in the driver app while the vehicle is moving.

## 9. Drugs and alcohol

Zero tolerance. Drivers doing US work are in the company's DOT drug and alcohol testing program. Refusing a test is treated as a positive result.

## 10. Speed

Company tractors are governed at 105 km/h. Tampering with the speed limiter is grounds for termination.

## 11. Passengers and pets

No passengers or pets without written approval from Safety & Compliance.

## 12. Incidents

Any collision, injury, spill, cargo damage, or roadside inspection must be reported. See C02 Accident & Incident Procedure.

## 13. Discipline

Violations are handled under the progressive discipline policy: verbal warning, written warning, suspension, termination. Serious violations (impairment, falsified logs, speed-limiter tampering, leaving the scene of a collision) may lead directly to termination.
