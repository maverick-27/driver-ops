# Dispatch SOP — what drivers need to know

## 1. Getting a load

Loads are assigned in the driver app. Accept or decline within **30 minutes**. If you decline, give a reason (hours, equipment, personal). Declines for hours-of-service reasons are always accepted.

## 2. Check calls

Send a status update in the driver app at each of these points:

- Arrived at shipper
- Loaded / departed shipper
- Crossed border (cross-border loads only)
- Arrived at receiver
- Empty / unloaded

If you are going to be **more than 1 hour late** for a pickup or delivery appointment, tell Dispatch as soon as you know.

## 3. Detention

If you wait more than **2 hours** at a shipper or receiver, send a "Detention" update with your arrival time. Maple Freight pays detention to drivers at the rate in the current pay schedule (see Payroll).

## 4. Breakdowns

See C06 Breakdown & Roadside Procedure. Do not arrange your own repairs unless the Breakdown desk tells you to.

## 5. Changing the plan

Never change a route, swap a trailer, or pick up an extra shipment without Dispatch approval in the app. Cross-border loads can never be changed at the driver's discretion; any change must go through the Border Desk.

## 6. ELD malfunction

If the ELD stops working, shows a malfunction, or won't record your status:

1. Note the time and what the device showed.
2. Tell Dispatch immediately through the driver app or by phone.
3. Start recording your hours on paper logs (blank logbook in the cab binder) from the time of the malfunction, and keep your logs for the current period available for inspection.
4. Dispatch will book a repair. Follow the legal time limits for repairing the device and for how long you may use paper logs in the jurisdiction you are in. Dispatch will confirm the limit that applies to your trip.
5. Give the paper logs to Safety & Compliance when you return to the yard.

## 7. Home time

Request home time at least **7 days** in advance in the app. Dispatch will plan loads to get you home on the requested date.
