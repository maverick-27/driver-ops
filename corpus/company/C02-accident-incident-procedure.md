# Accident & Incident Procedure

## 1. Scope

Applies to any collision (however minor), injury, cargo spill or damage, property damage, or roadside inspection involving a Maple Freight vehicle.

## 2. At the scene — every collision

Do these in order:

1. **Stop safely.** Never leave the scene of a collision. Turn on hazard lights.
2. **Check for injuries.** If anyone is hurt, or there is fire, a fuel leak, or a hazard to traffic, **call 911 first**.
3. **Protect the scene.** Put out warning triangles as you were trained. Wear your hi-vis vest outside the cab.
4. **Call Dispatch** at 905-555-0100. Dispatch will notify Safety & Compliance and insurance.
5. **Take photos** in the driver app: all vehicles from all four sides, damage close-ups, plates, the road and signs, weather and lighting.
6. **Exchange information** with the other party: name, licence number, plate, insurance company and policy number. Use the Collision Info form in the driver app.
7. **Get witness names and phone numbers** if anyone stopped.
8. **Do not admit fault**, sign anything other than police paperwork, or discuss the collision on social media.

## 3. Minor collision, no injuries

Follow section 2. In addition:

- If police do not attend, Ontario rules may require you to report the collision to a Collision Reporting Centre. Dispatch will tell you whether and where to report.
- Do not move the truck until Dispatch confirms it is safe and legal to continue, unless the truck is creating a hazard and police or the law direct you to move it.

## 4. Injury collision

Follow section 2. Do not move any injured person unless they are in immediate danger. Stay on scene until police release you.

## 5. Spill or hazardous load

Keep everyone back. Call 911 and Dispatch. If the load is dangerous goods, follow the emergency response information on your shipping documents.

## 6. Roadside inspection

Be polite and cooperative. Provide documents when asked. Send a photo of the inspection report to Dispatch in the driver app before you leave the inspection site. Out-of-service orders must be reported to Dispatch immediately.

## 7. After the incident

- Complete the Incident Report in the driver app within **24 hours**.
- Post-accident drug and alcohol testing may be required. Safety & Compliance will tell you.
- You may not drive again until Safety & Compliance clears you if the incident involved an injury or a police charge.

## 8. Near-misses

Report near-misses in the driver app. Near-miss reports are never used for discipline.
