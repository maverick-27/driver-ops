# Fuel Card & Expense Policy

## 1. Fuel card — allowed purchases

The company fuel card may be used **only** for:

- Diesel for the assigned company tractor
- Reefer fuel for an assigned company reefer trailer
- DEF (diesel exhaust fluid)
- Truck washes approved by Dispatch
- Scale tickets

**Not allowed on the fuel card:** food, drinks, personal items, cigarettes, lottery, cash advances, or fuel for any personal vehicle. Personal purchases on the fuel card are treated as theft.

## 2. Where to fuel

Fuel at in-network truck stops shown in the driver app whenever possible. Out-of-network fuelling needs Dispatch approval unless you are about to run out.

## 3. Per diem (meals)

Drivers on trips with an overnight away from the yard receive a meal per diem through payroll. Meals are **not** paid by fuel card and are **not** reimbursed by receipt. The per diem rate is in the current pay schedule.

## 4. Reimbursable expenses

Submit receipts in the driver app within **14 days**. Reimbursable:

- Parking and tolls not covered by the transponder
- Lumper fees approved by Dispatch
- Hotel, only when Dispatch books or approves it (e.g., truck out of service overnight)
- Minor supplies approved by the Breakdown desk (e.g., a fuse or bulb)

## 5. Lost or stolen card

Report a lost or stolen fuel card to Dispatch immediately (905-555-0100, 24/7). Dispatch will cancel it and issue a temporary fuel code.
