# Breakdown & Roadside Procedure

## 1. If the truck breaks down while driving

1. Signal and get off the travelled lanes as far as safely possible. On a highway, use the shoulder; take an exit if the truck can still move safely.
2. Turn on hazard lights.
3. Put on your hi-vis vest before leaving the cab.
4. Place warning triangles as you were trained.
5. **If you are in a dangerous spot** (live lane, blind curve, no shoulder, night with no lighting), call **911** first. Stay out of the truck if it may be struck; stand well away from traffic, behind a barrier if there is one.
6. Call the **Breakdown desk at 905-555-0177 (24/7)**. Give: unit number, exact location (highway, direction, nearest exit or km marker), what happened, whether the load is affected, and whether you are safe.
7. Send your location in the driver app.

## 2. What the Breakdown desk does

The Breakdown desk books an approved road-service or tow provider and gives you an ETA. **Do not call a tow truck or mechanic yourself** unless the Breakdown desk tells you to, or police order the truck moved.

## 3. While you wait

- Waiting time is on-duty time. Log it.
- Stay in contact with the Breakdown desk; update them if anything changes.
- Reefer loads: check the reefer temperature and report it.

## 4. Overnight

If the repair cannot be done the same night, the Breakdown desk or Dispatch will arrange a hotel. Hotel costs are reimbursed only when booked or approved by them (see C05, section 4).

## 5. Tires

A flat or damaged tire is a breakdown. Do not drive on a flat tire to "get to the next exit" unless the truck is in an unsafe location and you can move it at very low speed to a safer spot.
